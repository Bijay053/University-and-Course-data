## Doctor of Philosophy

Source:
<https://www.mq.edu.au/research/phd-and-research-degrees/explore-research-degrees/doctor-of-philosophy>

Captured title: **Doctor of Philosophy | Research degrees | Macquarie
University**

Relevant source excerpts:

> Your main pathway to a PhD at Macquarie University

> **Duration:** Three years full-time equivalent

> **Time commitment:** 35 hours per week (full time)

The page describes three PhD pathways, says the program is available in all
faculties, and links research-fee and scholarship information. Its fees and
scholarships section says scholarships may include one or more of:

- tuition fee offset;
- living allowance;
- top-up living allowance;
- fixed sum or one-off payment;
- Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) and/or visa application costs for
  international students.

## Fees authority

<https://www.mq.edu.au/study/admissions-and-entry/fees-and-costs/research-students>

The official research-fees page states that research-degree fees vary by
domestic/international status and explicitly says:

> If you are studying with us on an international student visa, you’re
> considered an international student and must pay international fees.

It separately identifies the Doctor of Philosophy/Master of Philosophy
research-degree fee structure and the domestic Research Training Program
funding. The public authority therefore supports treating PhD and MPhil as
internationally relevant research offerings; it does not require inventing
faculty-specific course variants.
