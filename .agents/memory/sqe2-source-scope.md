The standalone ULaw SQE2 Preparation Course's linked course-demands document is course-owned English evidence; requirements for the SQE2 postgraduate diploma or combined LLM are not substitutes.

**Why:** The course page omits English scores even though its linked demands document publishes them. The official explanatory blog describes five weeks of content but explicitly says completion takes longer; this is not a full-course duration.

**How to apply:** Resolve and parse the current linked document, retain its citation, and leave duration unknown unless current course-owned evidence explicitly establishes total length. Do not convert teaching content, exam dates, materials-access periods, or reviewer comments into duration.

A cited nonpublication review is an explanation of a missing value, not a successful numeric recovery or extra confidence. Suppress identical automatic attempts, but allow deliberate rechecking when new official evidence appears.

**Why:** The SQE2 sources can establish why five weeks is unsafe to use without establishing a replacement duration. Treating that review as a recovered value would mislead both reviewers and progress totals.

**How to apply:** Require successfully read official sources before asserting nonpublication; missing or failed fetches remain unverified. Preserve the citations through staging and clear the active status when authoritative duration is recovered.
