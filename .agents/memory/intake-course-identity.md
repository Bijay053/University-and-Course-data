---
name: Intake dates and course identity
description: The user's expectation for intake-specific rows of one course and safe consolidation.
---

Different intake months alone must not be presented as different awards. The user wants one course name without a date suffix, with its applicable intake months listed together.

**Why:** The user reported Advanced Computer Science MSc appearing three times for January, April and September, and the assistant repeatedly asked which course instead of recognising the consolidation request.

**How to apply:** Treat requests to combine intake-only variants as existing-record consolidation, not discovery or a fee-extraction-rule repair. Preserve genuine differences in award, campus, study mode, duration, tuition applicability and requirements. Equal fee amounts with different fee years are still a conflict to resolve, not permission to discard a cohort. Do not infer a missing stored intake solely from its URL.
