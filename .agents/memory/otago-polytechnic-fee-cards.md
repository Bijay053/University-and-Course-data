---
name: Otago Polytechnic fee cards
description: Audience and period semantics for tuition cards on Otago Polytechnic programme pages.
---

Otago Polytechnic programme pages render Domestic fees before International
fees. Select only the Standard card owned by the International fees section and
never use the lower scholarship card. Interpret “Full tuition” as a Full Course
amount; interpret “First year” and the doctorate-specific “Course one” as
Annual amounts.

**Why:** Flattened extraction selected the first Domestic amount. Treating every
International Standard card as Full Course then divided multi-year programme
fees by duration, creating false critically-low annual fees. The professional
doctorate uses “Course one” rather than “First year.”

**How to apply:** Scope the rule to typed Otago Polytechnic NZQA programme
routes. Keep the card label and fee period atomic, and fail to review rather
than substituting Domestic or scholarship amounts.
