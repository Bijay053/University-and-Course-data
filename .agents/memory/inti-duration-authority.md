name: INTI duration badge authority
description: Authoritative source and contamination risk for durations on INTI programme pages.
---

For `newinti.edu.my` programme pages, the non-loop duration badge in the top programme facts bar is authoritative. Ignore duration text inside related-product and compare-card containers.

**Why:** INTI embeds many sibling programme cards on every course page. Their one-, two-, and four-year values can beat or obscure the current programme’s duration in generic flattened-text extraction.

**How to apply:** Read the current badge before generic duration heuristics, exclude candidates with related/compare ancestors, preserve compound durations and minimum range semantics, and retain source-backed evidence for any production repair.
