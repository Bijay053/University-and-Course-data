---
name: UOW international study load
description: How to interpret UOW duration wording for international-course eligibility and study load.
---

For UOW international pages, a duration such as “2 years full-time or part-time equivalent” or “3 years, or part-time equivalent” has a full-time primary route and must be recorded as Full Time. A labelled duration that offers only part-time study is ineligible.

**Why:** UOW’s international page tooltip says part-time study is unavailable to international students studying onshore on a student visa. Choosing Part Time merely because both phrases appear misclassifies nearly the whole catalogue.

**How to apply:** Read the value paired with the Duration label. Prefer an explicit or implied primary full-time duration over a part-time-equivalent alternative, and reject only values that contain no full-time route.
