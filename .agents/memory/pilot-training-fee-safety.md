---
name: Pilot training cost fee safety
description: Distinguishes university tuition from separate aviation licensing and flight-training costs.
---

Amounts for additional flight training, licences, ratings, exams, or professional qualifications must never populate international tuition, even when they are larger and appear in a course Fees section.

**Why:** BNU pilot-course pages describe roughly £50,000–£60,000 of extra CPL/MER/UPRT/MCC licensing and training costs beside university tuition. Generic fee scoring preferred the larger amount.

**How to apply:** Reject these amounts across dated, structured, and generic fee paths. For BNU undergraduate pages with no explicit tuition after rejection, use the university's published annual international undergraduate fallback only as a fill-only value.
