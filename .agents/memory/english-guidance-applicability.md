Do not treat readable general guidance as proof that it applies to every course.
Keep undergraduate, postgraduate taught, health and postgraduate research
distinctions; retain unchecked rows as unverified and preserve conflicts.

**Why:** The user requires course-by-course applicability, particularly for
health and research exceptions, before bulk English fixes. LSBU source access
must never bypass robots restrictions.

**How to apply:** Bind scope and scores to exact official passages and actual
PDF pages, with accessible course identity evidence. General level statements
without explicit applicability are insufficient. Keep changes pending-only
and preserve separate publication approval and bulk confirmation.

Staff-uploaded documents can support approval-required proposals without being
authenticated as official website responses. Explicit document-wide level or
subject scope can be matched against stored course metadata without a live page;
course names need not match character-for-character.

**Why:** The user requires this across all universities, including general
requirements PDFs. Rejecting every upload or requiring exact names makes
authorised-document recovery unusable when the website is blocked.

**How to apply:** Retain staff-uploaded provenance in the preview and audit,
validate the actual PDF page and scope/score binding, label stored identity
evidence honestly, preserve conflicting existing values and health/research
exceptions, and leave unsupported level/subject matches unchanged. Never claim
the work complete until the production workflow has passed a live test.
