---
name: Catalogue recovery evidence
description: Distinguish recovery attempts from verified catalogue coverage.
---

Treat operator-reported counts and corrections as investigation inputs, not extraction authority or proof of catalogue completeness.

**Why:** A completed quality check can cover only discovered courses, while snapshot counts include repeated evidence and do not measure distinct programmes. Bounded recovery cannot certify a full catalogue.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing pending review rows, expose limits and exclusions, and require official source evidence before accepting corrections. Test the actual discovery path above fallback thresholds; counting sitemap URLs alone does not prove that a crawl consumes them.

Quick chat comparisons must distinguish observed official links from verified eligible courses, and approved, pending, historical and unrecorded inventory states. A bounded link comparison cannot certify complete catalogue coverage.

**Why:** The user wants actionable missing-course recovery rather than explanations, but treating a pending-only view or a fast website scan as a full inventory creates false missing-course claims.

**How to apply:** Keep the quick comparison explicitly partial, recheck selected candidates against university-wide inventory before confirmation dispatch, and offer the existing review-only fresh discovery flow for broader investigation. Never use an absent link to delete or hide an existing course.
