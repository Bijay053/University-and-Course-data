---
name: Catalogue recovery evidence
description: Distinguish recovery attempts from verified catalogue coverage.
---

Treat operator-reported counts and corrections as investigation inputs, not extraction authority or proof of catalogue completeness.

**Why:** A completed quality check can cover only discovered courses, while snapshot counts include repeated evidence and do not measure distinct programmes. Bounded recovery cannot certify a full catalogue.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing pending review rows, expose limits and exclusions, and require official source evidence before accepting corrections. Test the actual discovery path above fallback thresholds; counting sitemap URLs alone does not prove that a crawl consumes them.
