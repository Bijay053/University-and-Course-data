LSBU's headline international tuition card is the annual price, not the full-course total shown further down the same fees panel. The user confirmed that Civil Engineering's displayed £17,400 is per year. Do not turn that amount into a university-wide fallback: different courses have their own published prices, and blank international cards can genuinely be unpublished.

**Why:** Page-wide fee scanning selected a UK full-course total for Civil Engineering. Other correct international prices were rejected because their broad evidence windows also contained neighbouring home/UK labels. Research pages also contain writing-up prices and multi-year totals.

**How to apply:** Read the current course's owned international card or its full-time first-year international fee cell. Keep audience, currency and period atomic through later fallback passes. Evidence must exclude neighbouring UK columns. Never substitute part-time, writing-up, scholarship or full-course-total amounts for annual tuition, and do not guess a missing international price.
