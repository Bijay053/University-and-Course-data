---
name: ACAP international campus scope
description: Why ACAP marked campus restrictions must not reject an otherwise eligible degree.
---

ACAP's “not available to international students at indicated campuses” footnote applies only to marked availability options. An unmarked physical campus remains eligible; Online alone never proves eligibility.

**Why:** Bachelor of Criminology and Justice listed “Melbourne, Online^”. Treating its footnote as a whole-course exclusion dropped the degree, and page-wide campus scanning borrowed unrelated cities from navigation.

**How to apply:** Use the current course's labelled availability row, retain only unmarked physical campuses, and preserve genuine whole-course or all-marked exclusions. Keep the evidence attached to the actual course row rather than relying on generic international-fee navigation.

Research and repair should use a consistent, honestly identified source-verification network profile.

**Why:** On ACAP, an identified verifier retrieved current HTML while the generic Mozilla user-agent received HTTP 403 even with the same public-IP-pinned transport. A successful research fetch therefore did not prove the repair transport worked.

**How to apply:** Verify both transport paths against the same official course before diagnosing inaccessible content or asking the user to retry. Preserve DNS/peer checks, page/time budgets and fail-closed evidence validation.
