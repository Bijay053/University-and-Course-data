---
name: Canterbury programme-point fees
description: How Canterbury special programme fee amounts and point loads determine fee period and duration.
---

Treat University of Canterbury “Special Programme Fee” amounts paired with a point load as full-programme totals, not per-unit charges. Derive full-time duration at 120 points per year.

**Why:** Canterbury may show a programme amount such as NZ$68,850 beside “(180 points)” while separately listing the Student Services Levy as a genuine per-point charge. Generic nearby-text classification can let the levy wording incorrectly label the tuition fee Per Unit, and generic metadata can report 1 year for a 180-point programme.

**How to apply:** Pair the selected international tuition amount with its adjacent programme points, preserve its fee term as Full Course, set duration to points ÷ 120 years, and never use the separate SSL per-point wording to classify tuition.
