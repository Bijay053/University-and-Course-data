---
name: APU course delivery identity
description: How to distinguish APU Malaysia standard campus courses from explicit ODL courses.
---

For Asia Pacific University Malaysia, determine delivery and location from the current course identity rather than page-wide text. An explicit ODL marker in the course title, H1, or URL means Online with no physical campus; a standard APU course identity means On Campus in Kuala Lumpur.

**Why:** Every APU course page repeats online-learning navigation and a large related-course catalogue containing many ODL titles. Whole-page keyword matching marks ordinary campus programmes as Online. Once delivery is corrected, leaving campus unresolved triggers expensive AI fallback and per-course timeouts.

**How to apply:** Keep the rule restricted to APU course-detail URLs. Standard pages get Kuala Lumpur before AI/browser fallbacks. Explicit ODL pages retain an authoritative blank location through every fallback and remain excluded.
