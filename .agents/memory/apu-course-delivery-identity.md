---
name: APU course delivery identity
description: How to distinguish APU Malaysia standard campus courses from explicit ODL courses.
---

For Asia Pacific University Malaysia, determine delivery from the current course identity rather than page-wide text. An explicit ODL marker in the course title, H1, or URL means Online; a standard APU course identity means On Campus.

**Why:** Every APU course page repeats online-learning navigation and a large related-course catalogue containing many ODL titles. Whole-page keyword matching therefore marks ordinary campus programmes as Online and removes them at the online-only staging gate.

**How to apply:** Keep the rule restricted to APU course-detail URLs. Do not infer delivery from shared navigation or related-course text, and continue to reject course identities explicitly marked ODL.
