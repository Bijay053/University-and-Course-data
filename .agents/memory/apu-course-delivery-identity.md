For Asia Pacific University Malaysia, determine delivery and location from the current course identity rather than page-wide text. Read duration and fees only from the current course's dedicated facts and fee blocks.

**Why:** Every APU page repeats ODL navigation, related courses, alternate part-time durations, module durations, domestic fees, and indicative USD conversions. Page-wide parsing caused false Online classifications, AI timeouts, wrong duration units, and USD annual fees.

**How to apply:** Keep authority restricted to APU course-detail URLs. Standard pages are On Campus in Kuala Lumpur; explicit ODL pages retain a blank campus and remain excluded. Use the first duration in the live Course Details block and the international RM total as MYR/Full Course; reject hidden, related, comparison, and module blocks.
