---
name: Richmond online navigation noise
description: Prevent false online-only classification on Richmond American University London programme pages.
---

Richmond programme pages require strong, course-owned online-delivery evidence. Bare mentions of “online” must not classify the programme as online-only.

**Why:** Every page includes “Online Learning Resources” navigation, “Apply online,” and ordinary teaching or subject phrases such as “online discussion boards” and “online distribution.” These caused campus programmes to be rejected.

**How to apply:** Preserve labelled or structured declarations such as “Delivery mode: Online,” “fully online,” or equivalent explicit delivery wording. Suppress only the low-confidence bare-word fallback; do not disable the global online-only staging guard.
