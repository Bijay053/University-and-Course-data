name: Repair comparison and linked tuition
description: Why catalogue coverage percentages and domestic placeholders must not count as successful repairs.
---

Compare field changes on matching course identities, not percentages from different-sized samples. Missing baseline identity evidence is an unverified comparison.

**Why:** Lincoln's original 88 rows and a 31-row verification sample reported false IELTS/intake regressions and a false fee improvement without proving any course changed.

**How to apply:** Capture the baseline before verification replaces rows, preserve per-course presence through continuation, and keep absolute completeness separate from matched changes.

Course-owned fee links can contain the actual tuition when the course page contains none. International TBC does not authorize using a neighbouring UK amount, and research thesis-pending charges are not annual tuition.

**Why:** Lincoln's dental pages displayed UK £9,790 beside international TBC; research fee tables contained separate thesis-pending charges that generic money extraction selected.

**How to apply:** Validate the international/full-time intersection and its published year and period; use the same parser in repair validation and runtime recovery. Keep unpublished values empty rather than inventing them.
