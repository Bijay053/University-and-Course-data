# Acknowledgement of Country

Excelsia University College acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of Country, the Wallumattagal 
(or Wallumedegal) people of the Dharug Nation, on which our campus is situated. For countless 
generations, this clan consisted of fisher-hunter-gatherers in a rich environment of river flats, 
mangrove swamps, and creeks. Their name derives from the word ‘Wallumai’, or ‘Wallu’, meaning 
the Black Snapper fish (the local totem), which was once presumably plentiful in the Lane Cove River 
following on ‘Matta’, which refers to ‘place of’, and ‘Gal’, which refers to ‘People of’ Darug Nation.

**Excelsia also recognises the Wallumattagal People’s continuing connection to waters, 
lands, and sky and pays respect to their culture and Elders, past, present, and emerging.**
We identify and respect the continued living cultures of many remaining tribes of First Nations 
People, understanding that their Ancestors have walked kindly and cultivated this Country, and 
acknowledge their remarkable and distinctive place in our nation’s history, Australia: we know this 
land is, was and will always be Traditional Custodian’s land. We recognise that the continuation of 
language, family, culture, spiritual beliefs, and educational practices of Aboriginal and Torres Strait 
Islander Peoples is still as strong and holds as much significance today as before colonisation.

**Welcome**

Established in 1983, Excelsia University College is a leading Christian higher education provider 
in Australia. With our origins as a creative arts institution, today we form part of a dynamic global 
learning community with established partnerships and networks in Australia and across the world.

We are passionate about academic, professional, personal, and spiritual growth.

Offering undergraduate, postgraduate, research and short courses across business, counselling, 
creative and performing arts, education, information technology, English-language (ELICOS), and 
social work, we seek to foster an inclusive, connected community grounded in excellence, care, 
and purpose.

Excelsia is deeply committed to providing a 
world-class, holistic learning environment that 
equips students with the knowledge, skills, 
and confidence to thrive as leaders and 
change-makers in their chosen fields.

vibrant community.

The course is offered with two major strands of specialisation that 
students can choose depending on their passion and interests.

#### Performance practice

##### YEAR 1: FOUNDATIONS OF PERFORMANCE AND CRAFT

Your journey as an actor begins here. In your first year, you will lay the groundwork for a 
professional career in performance, developing essential skills in acting, voice, movement, and 
stagecraft. You will build confidence, explore your creative potential, and discover the discipline 
required to succeed in this industry.

##### YEAR 2: EXPANSION AND APPLICATION

Now that you have built a strong foundation, it’s time to expand your abilities. In your second year, 
you will deepen your practice with more complex performances, screen acting, and an introduction 
to directing.

##### YEAR 3: REFINEMENT AND PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

Your final year is all about refining your craft and preparing for the industry. This is where you push 
yourself to achieve professional excellence.

##### YOUR FUTURE STARTS HERE

By the end of this degree, you will have the skills, experience, and confidence to take your place in 
the world of performance – on stage, on screen, and beyond. Your career starts now.

#### Theatre practice

##### YEAR 1: FOUNDATIONS OF THEATRE AND CREATIVE EXPLORATION

Your journey as a theatre-maker begins here. In your first year, you will immerse yourself in the 
core principles of acting, directing, design, and stage management. Through hands-on workshops 
and collaborative projects, you will explore a range of theatrical techniques and gain insight into 
the diverse disciplines that bring a production to life. This foundational year will help you build 
confidence, refine your creative instincts, and develop the discipline required for a successful 
career in the performing arts.

##### YEAR 2: SPECIALISATION AND ARTISTIC DEVELOPMENT

With a strong foundation in place, your second year offers the opportunity to deepen your craft 
and expand your creative potential. You will take on more complex performances, develop your 
directing and producing skills, and refine your ability to craft original works. This year also allows 
for greater flexibility, enabling you to explore the areas of theatre-making that align with your 
artistic aspirations. Through hands-on productions and industry placements, you will begin shaping 
your unique voice as a theatre artist.

##### YEAR 3: PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE AND INDUSTRY READINESS

Your final year is about honing your expertise and preparing for a career in the industry. You will 
refine your skills in performance, directing, producing, and script development, while also gaining 
experience in real-world theatre environments. This year includes opportunities to work on major 
productions, develop independent projects, and establish professional networks. By the time you 
graduate, you will be fully equipped to make your mark in the industry – whether as a performer,
