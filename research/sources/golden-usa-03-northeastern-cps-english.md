## English Language Requirements

Non-native English speakers who are not subject to the test waiver policy are required to take one of the authorized proficiency exams below. Prospective students must achieve at least the minimum test score result to be considered for admission to their preferred program of study. Test results are valid for two years from the date of the exam.

Prospective students should self-report their test score results and upload a test-taker score report when prompted to do so on the application. If accepted, new students are required to arrange that their official scores be sent to Northeastern. All official test score results must be received directly from the testing institution.

**[Global Pathways Program](https://international.northeastern.edu/global-pathways/programs/)** Prospective students who take one of the authorized proficiency exams below and **do not** achieve the minimum test score requirements for admissions consideration may be eligible to apply to Northeastern’s Global Pathways program, which assists students to strengthen their academics, transition to American culture and an American classroom, and improve English skills.

### **English Exam Waiver**

**Prospective students who meet any of the following conditions are not required to take an English proficiency exam as part of the application process:**

- Conferred associate’s, bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree from the list of approved English speaking countries: Anguilla, Antigua, Australia, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands, Canada (except Quebec), Cayman Islands, Dominica, Ghana, Grenada, Greenland, Guyana, Jamaica, Kenya, Liberia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Republic of Ireland, St. Kitts and Nevis, St. Lucia, South Africa, Tobago, Tanzania, Trinidad, Turks, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe, and Caicos Islands, United Kingdom, U.S.A, US Virgin Islands
- Conferred associate’s, bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree from any institution where English is the only medium of instruction
- Enrolled in the final semester of a degree program in one of the aforementioned countries

#### **TOEFL:** _Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL Code for CPS: 4999)_

Internet-based and measures four areas of English language skills: speaking, listening, writing, and reading.

The College of Professional Studies only accepts TOEFL results from the ETS database. Please use institution code 4999.

For exams taken **before January 21, 2026** we require a minimum TOEFL iBT score of:

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED** |
| --- | --- |
| All Doctoral Programs | 100 |
| All other graduate programs | 79 |
| Bachelor’s Completion | 79 |

For exams taken **on or after January 21, 2026** we require a minimum TOEFL iBT score of:

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED** |
| --- | --- |
| All Doctoral Programs | 5.5 |
| All other graduate programs | 4 |
| Bachelor’s Completion | 4 |

For more information about the TOEFL iBT Score Breakdown please review the link here: [https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html)

#### **Academic IELTS:** _International English Language Testing System_

Measures ability to communicate in English across listening, reading, writing, and speaking skills.

The College of Professional Studies only accepts IELTS results delivered to our Electronic Download account by the IELTS test center. An institution code for IELTS is NOT required. Please contact the test center where you took the IELTS test directly, and request that your test scores be sent to the account name: _Northeastern University College of Professional Studies._

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED** |
| --- | --- |
| Graduate | 6.5 |
| All Doctoral Programs | 7 no band lower than a 6.5 |
| Bachelor’s Completion | 6.5 |

#### **Duolingo English Test**

[Duolingo English Test](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/) is a modern English proficiency assessment available online, on demand. The exam can be taken from home via a computer and webcam and is administered using computer adaptive technology, meaning that the question difficulty adapts to each test taker. The test also integrates a video interview and writing sample, all of which are sent to the institution for final assessment. The entire test experience takes just under an hour and test results are certified within 48 hours.

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED**(ON 160-POINT SCALE) | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED**(ON 100-POINT SCALE) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate | 105 | 48 |
| All Doctoral Programs | 120 | 68 |
| Bachelor’s Completion | 105 | 48 |

#### **LanguageCert Academic**

[LanguageCert Academic Test](https://www.languagecert.org/en/language-exams/english/languagecert-academic/academic) is an English proficiency assessment available both online and in-person that measures the ability to communicate in four areas of skills: reading, writing, listening, and speaking. The online option uses a combination of AI as well as live proctoring and can be taken from home via a computer with a webcam.

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED** |
| --- | --- |
| All Graduate Programs and Bachelor’s Completion Programs (excluding Doctoral) | 75 |
| All Doctoral Programs | 90 |

#### **Oxford ELLT**

[Oxford ELLT English Test](https://oxfordellt.com/ellt/) is a modern English proficiency assessment available online, on demand, that uses a combination of AI and live proctoring to measure the ability to communicate in four areas of skills: reading, writing, listening, and speaking. The exam can be taken from home via a computer with a webcam, and test results are certified within 48 hours.

| **PROGRAM** | **MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED** |
| --- | --- |
| All Graduate Programs and Bachelor’s Completion Programs (excluding Doctoral) | 7 |
| All Doctoral Programs | 9 |
