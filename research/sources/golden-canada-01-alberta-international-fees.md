## Instructional Fees

#### Graduate instructional fees differ for **[Canadian](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/tuition-fees/instructional-fees-domestic.html) and international students**; they also vary according to the **date of admission** and the **type of program** (course-based or thesis-based). To determine your instructional fees for the 2026-27 academic year, locate your registration status, admission date and program type in the lists below. On this website, we provide fee estimates for your information; your actual fee assessments are available on [Bear Tracks](https://www.beartracks.ualberta.ca/) after you register.

#### Non-standard Fees

Please note that it is not possible to include fee information for all programs or courses here, as some have a **non-standard fee structure**. If your program has a non-standard fee structure, you can find it on the [non-standard fees list](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/tuition-fees/non-standard.html).

If you are unsure about your program's fee structure or you do not see your program on the non-standard fees list, please contact your department.

Find out how to contact the department by [finding your program page »](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-programs/index.html)

If this applies to you, please [contact GPS](https://universityofalberta.freshservice.com/support/catalog/items/379?_gl=1*i3vqqi*_gcl_au*Mjg2MjcxNjI5LjE3NTE5MDQ3NjQ.*_ga*NjY0NDQyMDc3LjE3NDQwMzY1MTI.*_ga_21TWH2P5G7*czE3NTYxNTgyMzQkbzE2MyRnMSR0MTc1NjE1OTEyNCRqNDQkbDAkaDU4MDY4OTA5OQ..) for more information.

Note that it is very important to **check your [Bear Tracks](https://www.beartracks.ualberta.ca/) account regularly**. Due to system configuration and changes, student fees may be recalculated throughout the term. Please notify [GPS](https://universityofalberta.freshservice.com/support/catalog/items/379?_gl=1*i3vqqi*_gcl_au*Mjg2MjcxNjI5LjE3NTE5MDQ3NjQ.*_ga*NjY0NDQyMDc3LjE3NDQwMzY1MTI.*_ga_21TWH2P5G7*czE3NTYxNTgyMzQkbzE2MyRnMSR0MTc1NjE1OTEyNCRqNDQkbDAkaDU4MDY4OTA5OQ..) as soon as possible if you are concerned about your fee assessment being either too high or too low in value.

Finally, please note that in addition to paying instructional fees, **students must also pay [non-instructional fees](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/tuition-fees/non-instructional.html)** (most of them mandatory). For information on non-instructional fees, visit [here](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/tuition-fees/non-instructional.html).

## Instructional Fees for International Students

For the purposes of the following, _Full Time_ refers to registration in 9 or more credits per term in the Fall/Winter terms and 6 or more credits in the Spring/Summer terms. _Part Time_ refers to registration in less than 9 credits per term in the Fall/Winter terms and less than 6 credits per term in the Spring/Summer terms.

The _Admitted in Fall 2020_ _and Beyond_ section below refers to admission as part of the **Fall 2022 cohort (that is, admission in Fall 2022, Winter 2023, Spring 2023, or Summer 2023).**

Note that in Spring/Summer terms, students in thesis-based programs who choose to enrol in a NON-Thesis course for credit or audit will be charged additional fees.

### For visiting international students admitted into Research 900:

## Research 900 (for Visiting Graduate Students)

Research 900 (RSCH 900) is offered as a course registration for **_visiting graduate_** **_students_** who are registered at another academic institution and who will be conducting research **only** at the University of Alberta under the supervision of a UofA faculty member, but will not be taking UAlberta courses.

Research 900 students must be continuously registered for each term they are on campus (ie. each Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer term) to maintain their student status and campus privileges.

| #### 2026-27 Fees (in Canadian dollars) |
| --- |
| **Fall** | **Winter** | **Spring** | **Summer** |
| $413.18 | $413.18 | $413.18 | $413.18 |

In addition to these instructional fees, Research 900 students pay [Non-Instructional Fees](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-studies/fees-funding/tuition-fees/non-instructional.html); the combined fees provide them with a ONECard and access to resources and services such as athletic and recreation facilities, health services, WCB and liability coverage, U-Pass, and official UAlberta transcript certifying attendance.

**Note:** Visiting Graduate Students in RSCH 900 in Fall or Winter terms may opt into the GSA Health and Dental plan offered by Studentcare Networks. Contact [Alumo (formerly Studentcare)](https://www.studentcare.ca/) for details.
