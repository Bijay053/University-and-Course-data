Table 2: Typical international undergraduate course tuition fees for 
2026

The fees in Table 2 provide an estimate of the course tuition fees for students commencing an undergraduate course in Semester 1 
2026. As far as possible, the fees provided in this table show the typical tuition fees that a student enrolled in each undergraduate 
course will pay for the first year of the course in 2026. As a guide, a typical total course fee is also shown.

For most courses, the typical tuition fees are based on subject enrolments normally undertaken by students in the course and reflect 
the discipline band of the subjects comprising the degree. For some degrees, a typical fee range is shown, due to the wide range of 
variables that may affect the course structure and subjects taken.

Please note that:

• The fees provided are indicative only and may vary depending on the number of subjects undertaken, their credit point value, and 
the discipline band for each subject undertaken during your enrolment. Actual tuition fees payable may vary and will be the sum
of the fees for all subjects in which you enrol.

• The 2026 typical course fee shown provides an estimate of the tuition fees for the first year of the course. This estimate assumes a 
full-time study load, commencing in Semester 1, and is for 2026 only. If, for example, you complete a subject sequence in one year 
that is more than one EFTSL, your fee liability will increase.

• The typical total course fee shown provides an estimate of the tuition fees for the whole course. This estimate is based on typical 
subject enrolments in each year of the course with indexation of the 2026 international undergraduate subject fees at an estimate 
of annual fee increases over the duration of the course based on the maximum annual course tuition increase specified in the 
Student Loans, Fees and Charges Policy (MPF 1325). The amount shown assumes students complete no more than the course 
duration indicated.
