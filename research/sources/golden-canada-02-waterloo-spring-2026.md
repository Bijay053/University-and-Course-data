# Master's and PhD program tuition Spring 2026 - International Students

## How tuition is charged

At University of Waterloo the master’s tuition rates are assessed in a variety of ways, including:

- Residency status (domestic, domestic out of province, or international)
- Enrolment status (full-time, part-time)
- Research/coursework programs (including additional fees for specialized MENG/MMSc programs)
- Online professional graduate programs
- On campus professional graduate programs
- Some programs are course-based, and there is charge per course

University of Waterloo charges tuition one term at time, with three terms occurring over the course of a year:

- Fall Term – September to December
- Winter Term – January to April
- Spring Term – May to August

[Non-tuition fees](https://uwaterloo.ca/finance/student-financial-services/incidental-fees) are also charged to your student account on a termly basis.

### Other tuition fee assessments

- **Graduate diploma** – tuition fees for students who are registered in a graduate diploma program differ than those of a master’s program (see alphabetical program listing below for costs)
- **Non-degree** graduate students - pay the research/coursework master's rate indicated below, plus incidental fees.  Non-degree graduate students in professional programs pay tuition consistent with their program plus incidental fees.
- **Qualifying graduate students -** pay fees at the year two undergraduate per-course fee corresponding to their program. For more information, please see the [undergraduate fee schedule](https://uwaterloo.ca/finance/student-financial-services/tuition-fee-schedules).

### Other possible charges

- A charge for residence may also be billed to your student account. For detailed information on refunds for housing charges, please see [Campus Housing](https://uwaterloo.ca/campus-housing/applications).

**Graduate co-operative programs:**

2026/2027 Co-op Fee: **$836** will be charged to students using the service of [Co-operative and Experiential Education (CEE)](https://uwaterloo.ca/co-operative-education/your-co-op-fee).

- Co-op fees represent the cost of running the co-op program, spread out over a specific number of terms. They are associated with your enrollment in the co-op program and are not “placement fees” or “internship fees”.
- Grad co-op fees are assessed on the program's first study term, with the remaining fee(s) assessed during each subsequent work term.
- Students admitted to a co-op program prior to fall 2025 will be charged the co-op fee only when enrolled in a work term.
- Check out the co-op fee frequently asked questions ( [FAQs](https://uwaterloo.ca/co-operative-education/your-co-op-fee/co-op-fee-faqs)) to learn more about the cost associated with enrolling in a co-op program.

### Tuition and residence refunds

- Withdrawal or program completion effective on or before the 100% refund deadline will result in all tuition and co-op charges for the term being removed.
- Withdrawal or program completion effective on or before the 50% refund deadline will result in a 50% adjustment to the charges for tuition, student services fees and co-op fee only.
