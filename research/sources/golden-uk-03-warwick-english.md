##### English Language Requirements

If you are a non-native English speaker we need to confirm that your English language competency is sufficiently strong for you to get the most out of your Master's study. We therefore require you to take a SELT (Secure English Language Test) and submit your results as one of your academic requirements.

Below you will find a table of accepted English tests showing the minimum grades required. You will need a balanced score across all sections, and the test must have been taken within two years of starting your MSc course.

###### Exemptions

You may sufficiently satisfy the English language requirements of your course if:

- You are a national of an English speaking country recognised by [UK Visa & Immigration (UKVI)Link opens in a new window](https://www.gov.uk/tier-4-general-visa/knowledge-of-english)

Or

- You have been educated entirely in English, in a UKVI recognised English speaking country for one or more years and completed this qualification within the last 2 years.

If you do not qualify for an exemption and are offered a place on the course, your offer will be conditional on providing a suitable English qualification.

Note that exemptions are applied solely at the discretion of the Admissions Team and **we reserve the right to require an English test result from any student.**

##### Requirement for direct entry onto MSc ( _no pre-sessional English required_)

WMG Courses are Band A as standard, **with the exception of MSc Innovation and Entrepreneurship** which is Band B (see the 2nd table)

For direct entry onto the MSc, you must achieve at least the following overall and component scores for Band A courses:

| Test | Overall | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IELTS Academic | **6.5** | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ |
| IELTS for UKVI | **6.5** | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ |
| IELTS Online | **6.5** | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ | 6.0+ |
| PTE (Academic) | **62** | 59+ | 59+ | 59+ | 59+ |
| PTE Academic UKVI | **62** | 59+ | 59+ | 59+ | 59+ |
| Duolingo | **120** | 110+ | 110+ | 110+ | 110+ |
| TOEFL(iBT) and TOEFL (iBT) Special Home Edition<br>_Tests taken before 21st January 2026_ | **92** | 21+ | 22+ | 21+ | 23+ |
| TOEFL (iBT) and TOEFL (iBT) Special Home Edition<br>_Tests taken on or after 21st January 2026_ | **4.5** | 4.5+ | 4.5+ | 4.5+ | 4.5+ |
| CAE | **180**<br>**(Grade C)** | 3 x borderline, 1 x weak <br>No component below 170 |
| CPE | **180**<br>**(Grade C)** | 4 x pass grades (at any level) <br> <br>No component below 170 |
| Trinity ISE II | **Distinction Distinction Merit Merit in four components** |
| Trinity ISE III | **Pass in all four components** |
| Trinity ISE IV | **Pass in all four components** |

- We do not accept the Pearson online test.
- We do not accept the IELTS _One Skill Retake._ You must achieve the required scores in all four components in one test.
- Scores must have been achieved in a single test.
- The test must have been taken within two years of starting your MSc
- Only your most recent test will be considered.\*
- For Duolingo, your test result must include the speaking, listening, reading and writing component scores. Older tests without these components cannot be accepted.

_If you have taken an English test multiple times, **please only submit the most recent test result**. Your conditions of offer will always be based on your most recent test, and may be modified if you submit additional tests after receiving an offer._

**If you are applying for a TOEFL iBT test you will need to enter an Institution Code - for Warwick this is 0806**

##### Requirement for MSc entry with pre-sessional English (PSE)

If you narrowly miss the English standards required, you may be able to attend the Pre-sessional English Course (PSE) in order to clear your English condition. There are [6 week](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/courses/6-week-presessional-english/) and [10 week](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/courses/10-week-presessional-english/) on-campus options available, plus a [6 week online course](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/courses/6-week-presessional-english-online/). Please check the [Pre-Sessional site](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/) carefully for English score requirements.

- For 6 and 10 week on-campus courses, students who are studying on all WMG MSc courses **except Innovation and Entrepreneurship** should [select the Band A Postgraduate Courses tab on this screen](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/entry-requirements/) for the required English test scores.
- For 6 and 10 week on-campus courses, students studying MSc Innovation and Entrepreneurship should select the [Band B Postgraduate Courses](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/entry-requirements/) tab for their required English test scores for the on-campus courses.
- For those wishing to study the [online Pre-sessional course](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/short-courses/pre-sessional-english/courses/6-week-presessional-english-online/), students studying Innovation and Entprepreneurship are required to meet the _Band B requirements_. All other WMG MSc students are required to meet the general " _WMG Postgraduate Courses_" requirements.

[Full information about the pre-sessional English course, eligibility criteria and application form can be found here.Link opens in a new window](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/learn-english/pre-sessional/wmg6week)

If you have any questions related to the pre-sessional English course, please contact the team at [pseadmissions@warwick.ac.uk](mailto:pseadmissions@warwick.ac.uk) before you submit your application.

Further information relating to English Language requirements for the Student Route Visa can be found on our [Immigration and Compliance pagesLink opens in a new window](https://warwick.ac.uk/study/international/immigration/student_route/english).
